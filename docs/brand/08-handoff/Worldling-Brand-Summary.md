# Worldling brand summary

**Grow a world of your own.**  
Visit everyone else's.

**Cozy cosmic.** The vastness of space, made small enough to hold. Create a living 3D planet, visit a shared universe and leave a comet: a short, kind message.

**Personality:** wondrous, warm, calm, curious, kind and crafted.

## The sprouting world

Solid planet. The ring rises 19 degrees toward the right. The right leaf is slightly larger and lifted. Preserve the even transparent front gap. Use the supplied SVGs and outlined wordmark. Clear space is one quarter of the planet diameter. Minimum widths: master mark 48px, horizontal lockup 160px, stacked lockup 128px. Use the optical favicon versions at 32px and 16px. No stretching, arbitrary colors, effects or busy backgrounds.

## Type and interface

Bricolage Grotesque for display, mainly weight 650, with a fluid 40-72px display style. Geist for body and UI: body 16-18px, labels 500 and buttons 600. Geist Mono for standalone data and timestamps. Measurements assume a 16px root.

Use a 4px spacing grid, radii of 8px, 16px, 24px and full, a 48px main-action height and 44px icon targets. Glass is 88% Deep Space with 20px blur and an opaque fallback.

## Worlds and motion

Painterly miniature dioramas with soft upper-left light, matte terrain and satin oceans. Use quiet stars, faint dust and fine grain. Keep the mood intimate and calm.

Feedback 150ms; panels 240ms; world focus 600ms; comet 900ms; bloom 1200ms; full drift cycle 16 seconds. Movement is slow and smooth. Offer pause. Reduced motion uses fades and static views.

## Voice

- Make wonder feel close.
- Be clear when it matters.
- Invite people at their own pace.
- Celebrate care and connection.
- Use warm everyday language.

Avoid guilt, false urgency and claims of success before the app confirms them.

French uses friendly **tu**. Tagline: **Fais grandir un monde à ton image.** Secondary line: **Découvre les mondes des autres.** Growth label: **Niveau de floraison**.
